AI across the work cycle

Choose the stage that matches the job. Add deal context, source boundaries and the required format. Treat every output as working material until a named human has reviewed it.

01

Review

AI as analyst · organise the inbound
1.1Extract & summarise

Identify the ten facts that matter most to this transaction. Cite the page or source for each and explain why it matters.

1.2Classify the evidence

Separate confirmed facts, management claims, assumptions and unanswered questions. Do not convert claims into facts.

1.3Focus attention

What deserves immediate human attention before we analyse the rest? Rank by potential deal impact and explain the trigger.

02

Structure

AI as thought partner · organise the approach
2.1Define the decision

What decision must the reader make, what do they need to believe, and what evidence is required to support that belief?

2.2Develop the thesis

Propose three alternative structures for the core argument. Show supporting evidence, weaknesses and the question each leaves open.

2.3Plan the output

Turn the agreed thesis into a page plan: headline, supporting evidence, exhibit, source and unresolved question for each page.

03

Create

AI as co-author · produce a controlled first pass
3.1Draft the first pass

Draft for a sceptical investment-banking audience. Distinguish sourced evidence, inference and open items; preserve every material caveat.

3.2Generate alternatives

Give me three materially different options: factual, commercially persuasive and contrarian. Keep each supportable from the source pack.

3.3Tighten & improve

Reduce length by 30% without changing numbers, deleting caveats or strengthening conclusions. List every substantive edit.

04

Challenge

AI as counterparty · find weakness before release
4.1Red-team the argument

Review this as a hostile buyer trying to reduce price. What would you challenge, what alternative explanation fits, and what evidence would answer it?

4.2Verify support

Identify every unsupported claim, number requiring tie-out, inconsistent period, missing source and conclusion stronger than its evidence.

4.3Simulate the recipient

How would a strategic buyer, PE sponsor and lender read this differently? What would each question, discount or need proven?
